Current website inventory

The website code currently sets no cookies and uses no localStorage or sessionStorage. It includes no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, third-party fonts or embedded video. The video shown on the homepage is a local placeholder. No optional tracking consent banner is needed for that current implementation.

The consultation form temporarily holds your entries and selected date/time in browser memory. They are not saved across reloads by the application. The form contacts the website's consultation endpoint and, on submission, the server sends the request to Telegram. This is enquiry processing, not a browser tracking cookie; it is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Hosting and third-party services

The repository is configured for Vercel hosting. The host may process request and security information, and infrastructure or deployment protection may behave differently from the website code. The live domain, hosting configuration and any infrastructure cookies have not been audited here. Operator action pending: verify the live site and document any cookie name, provider, purpose, duration and consent basis before enabling optional features.

Telegram is called from the server and is not embedded in your browser. If a separate booking service is configured, clicking its link takes you to a service with its own privacy and cookie practices. This page does not claim that external sites are cookie-free.

If optional tracking is introduced

Analytics, advertising, tracking pixels and video embeds that access device information may require consent under Dutch/EU rules. They must be assessed before activation. Where consent is required, keep them blocked until an affirmative choice; provide clear purpose and provider information, an equally accessible reject option and a way to change or withdraw the choice as easily as accepting. Continuing to browse is not consent.

Strictly necessary functionality can have a different consent rule; the Dutch exception for some low-impact analytics must be assessed for the specific configuration. Calling a tool “analytics” does not itself exempt it. A cookie banner cannot replace lawful data processing or accurate disclosures.

Your controls and email tracking

You can inspect, block or delete site data in your browser settings. There are currently no optional website tracking choices to change because none are implemented. If that changes, this page must link to working consent controls.

Restaurant email open pixels and tracked links are separate from this website. They are not implemented in this repository. If used in a campaign, the restaurant must explain the actual tracking and assess its legal basis and any consent requirement; subscribing to emails should not be treated as automatic consent to every tracking purpose.
