Roles and status of this notice

A restaurant normally controls its guest newsletter: it determines the audience, purpose, signup offer and campaigns. DineAgain acts as processor when carrying out those tasks only on the restaurant's documented instructions. DineAgain separately controls its own enquiries and administration. Roles follow actual activities, not just labels, and must be reviewed if purposes are decided jointly.

This is an informational notice, not an executed data processing agreement (DPA). Before handling real guest data, the restaurant and the identified DineAgain legal entity must enter into a binding agreement meeting Article 28 GDPR. Operator identity, actual providers and security arrangements remain to be confirmed.

Processing to document

The DPA must specify its subject matter and duration; the nature and purposes of processing; the guest/subscriber categories and data types; and the restaurant's rights and obligations. The proposed activities are QR signup collection, storing consent evidence, managing subscriber lists, sending approved welcome/promotional emails, handling unsubscribes and providing agreed reports.

Typical data may include email, optional name/preferences, restaurant and signup source, consent record and suppression status. Delivery, open or click records should be listed only if actually used and lawful. Do not upload health, allergy or other sensitive data as routine marketing-list fields. Decide and record retention periods and minimise collected information.

Required safeguards and assistance

  • Process only on documented instructions, including instructions about international transfers; address unlawful instructions and applicable legal requirements.
  • Bind authorised people to confidentiality and use appropriate technical and organisational security measures matched to the risk.
  • Obtain the restaurant's prior specific or general written authorisation for subprocessors, with notice and a meaningful opportunity to object where general authorisation applies; impose equivalent obligations on them.
  • Assist with guest rights requests, security duties, breaches, impact assessments and regulatory consultations as applicable. Notify the restaurant of personal data breaches without undue delay; agree an operational notification channel and response process.
  • Provide compliance information and permit appropriate audits and inspections.
  • At the restaurant's choice, return or delete data at the end of services unless law requires retention; document treatment of backups, copies and suppression records.
